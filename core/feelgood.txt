irst: what actually makes “hitting a symbol” feel good?
Not visuals.
Prediction → confirmation → amplification.
If you don’t get those three phases right, no amount of glow, particles, or shaders will help.
The 3-phase loop (non-negotiable)
Anticipation
“This might hit”
Confirmation
“Yes, it hit”
Reward amplification
“Oh damn, that hit hard”
Balatro nails this with timing, sound, and micro-movement, not fireworks.
Core rule (I’m serious about this):
Movement beats particles. Sound beats movement. Timing beats everything.
Particles come last.
Let’s design this properly for a 2D slot roguelike
I’ll give you concrete, implementable layers, ordered by impact.
LAYER 1 — Symbol motion (highest ROI)
When a symbol hits, it must react.
❌ Bad
Symbol stays static
Score number pops up
Some particles appear
✅ Good
Each hit symbol does one decisive motion:
Micro punch scale
scale up → snap back (≤120ms)
Directional nudge
slightly push outward from the center of the hit pattern
Rotation jitter (±2–4° max)
This gives physicality.
libGDX tip
Use non-linear easing:
ease-out-back
ease-out-elastic (tiny)
Linear interpolation feels dead.
LAYER 2 — Hit freeze (the secret sauce)
Balatro does this constantly.
Add a hit stop:
Freeze everything for 30–80 ms
Resume with force
This creates:
weight
impact
“oh shit” moments
No particles required.
Even a single frame freeze is noticeable.
LAYER 3 — Symbol isolation (focus beats chaos)
When a pattern hits:
Dim or desaturate non-hit symbols
Slightly brighten or sharpen hit symbols
Optional: subtle vignette
This tells the player:
This is what mattered.
Your brain rewards clarity more than spectacle.
LAYER 4 — Score reveal choreography (not numbers)
Don’t just show “+120”.
Do this instead:
Numbers count up
They appear after the hit stop
Font weight increases with multiplier tiers
Slight screen shake ONLY when crossing thresholds
Example:
Pair → no shake
Triple → light shake
Rare combo → directional shake + freeze
This builds escalation.
LAYER 5 — Sound design (this is where dopamine lives)
I know you didn’t ask, but I won’t let you ignore this.
Rules:
Each symbol type has a timbre
Combos stack pitch, not volume
Multipliers add harmonics, not new sounds
Balatro is basically a musical instrument.
If your audio is bad, visuals won’t matter.
LAYER 6 — Particles (now they finally matter)
Use particles sparingly and purposefully:
Good particle usage:
Burst outward from hit symbols
Directional, short-lived
Color tied to symbol identity
Appear AFTER hit stop
Bad particle usage:
Constant ambient sparkle
Large slow fades
Generic glow everywhere
Particles should feel like debris, not fog.
LAYER 7 — Depth & layering (since you asked)
Yes, layering helps — but only if meaningful.
Simple but effective depth stack:
Background (dark, stable)
Reel/symbol layer
Hit symbols (temporary z-boost)
Particles
Score / UI overlay
Screen-space effects (shake, flash)
During a hit:
Lift hit symbols forward (z or draw order)
Push everything else back visually
Depth = hierarchy, not blur.
One thing I’ll explicitly warn you against
Do NOT animate everything on every spin.
If everything moves:
Nothing feels special
Big hits feel the same as small hits
Reserve intensity for rarity.
